An Axelrod tournament for the parables

Faith Theory

In 1980 Robert Axelrod asked game theorists for strategies in a repeated game of trust. The simplest entry, Tit for Tat, beat them all. This lab runs the same kind of tournament, but every player is a kind of faith drawn from Scripture: steadfast, discerning, undulating, small as a mustard seed, lukewarm, or none at all. Each one is tested twice, once under the sun with only the game, and once under heaven with four of God's promises written into the rules.

  1. Under the sun, Joseph wins. Leading with trust, answering wrongs, and forgiving about one wrong in three scores highest and grows to more than half of the population within 60 generations. Tit for Tat (Eye for an Eye) comes second.

    Gen 50:20 · Matt 10:16

  2. Once mistakes happen, forgiveness beats strict justice. With 5% of moves misfiring, Eye for an Eye falls from 2nd to 7th as single errors set off endless retaliation, while the forgiving strategies hold the top two places.

    Eccl 9:11 · Matt 18:22

  3. Undulating faith sinks on its own. A community of wavering believers drifts down to 8% cooperation with no outside help, and a wavering agent scores the same whatever faith it starts with. Under heaven it is held up near 55%, though it is never healed.

    James 1:6-8 · Matt 14:31

  4. Turning the other cheek pays only under heaven. Steadfast faith is last under the sun. It becomes first once heaven repays about a third of each wrong (s ≈ 0.3), and the promise that does this is reaping.

    Matt 5:39 · Rom 12:19

  5. No faith wins early and loses late. Cain gains share for the first few generations, then is cut down to almost nothing as the people he exploited disappear.

    Ps 37:1-2 · Ps 73:17

The rules

The Covenant Game

Every step, every player meets every other player once and makes one choice: keep covenant (act in good faith) or betray. These are the payoffs Axelrod used.

Your moveThey keep covenantThey betray
You keep covenant3Blessing for both0Betrayed (they get 5)
You betray5Spoil (they get 0)1Famine for both

Each player carries a faith level between 0 and 1: the chance that it actually does the good its conscience recommends. Faith without works is dead, so faith only counts here when it turns into a move. Faith is also carried from one relationship to the next, so being betrayed by one neighbour can change how you treat all the others.

Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.James 2:17

Two worlds

Under the sun is the game and nothing else. Whatever happens comes from the players meeting each other. Under heaven adds four promises, each turned up by one dial s from 0 (absent) to 1 (in full force). The default is 0.5.

Unity

When both keep covenant, the blessing grows from 3 to 3 + s.

Ps 133:1, 3

Reaping

Ten steps after a betrayal, heaven settles a share s of it. The betrayed get back s of what they lost, but only if they have not avenged themselves. The betrayer gives back s of what they gained, unless they have been reconciled by then.

Gal 6:7-9 · Rom 12:19 · Matt 5:24

Restoration

Faith anchored in God is pulled back toward its anchor every step, so it does not fail.

Ps 23:3 · Luke 22:32

Renewal

When you do wrong and are met with good, your heart softens and your faith rises. This works for anyone, even the hardened.

Ezek 36:26 · Rom 12:20-21

What the heaven results mean. The four promises are modelled as written here. The heaven results show what follows if they work this way. They are a thought experiment and do not measure God. The results under the sun come only from the game.

Reaping alone is enough to lift Steadfast from last to first, and it sends Cain to the bottom. Unity rewards anyone who reciprocates, so Joseph leads. Renewal partly redeems the faithless and warms the lukewarm. Restoration does least by itself, because it only holds faith steady and does not change what faith earns.

Test 4

Levels of faith, face to face

Here faith is just a level. A focused faith holds its level whatever it sees. An undulating faith starts at its level, rises and falls with its own tide, and is pushed by how it is treated. Every level meets every other in pairs, and the diagonal is each level with its own kind.

Test 5

The faith curve

One newcomer is dropped into the full tournament at each faith level, from none to full. How well does it do?

Focused faith: under the sun, faith barely pays until it is nearly whole. From 0% to 70% the score moves by only a few hundredths, and the real gain is between 80% and 100%. Under heaven every step of faith pays, and full faith earns a third more than none.

Undulating faith: under the sun the line is flat. Wherever it starts, the wind carries it to the same low place (about 0.3), so its starting faith makes no difference. Under heaven it is held up and its level counts again, though it never matches the focused line.

For he that wavereth is like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed. … A double minded man is unstable in all his ways.James 1:6, 8

World

Test 6

Generations

Axelrod's ecological tournament: after each round, every archetype's share of the population grows in proportion to its score. All eleven start at 9.1% and run for 60 generations.

Under the sun, Joseph takes over (55%), and Tit for Tat, the Prodigal and the Mustard Seed survive beside him. No Faith grows a little at first and then dies out: once the trusting are gone, there is nobody left to exploit. Rocky Ground dies out as well, and Thorny Ground dwindles to about 1%.

Under heaven no single kind takes over. Steadfast and Joseph lead, and the Lukewarm, Prodigal and Mustard Seed share the rest. Strict justice (Eye for an Eye) and Show Me First fade away.

Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity. For they shall soon be cut down like the grass.Psalm 37:1-2

Test 7

Peradventure ten

A city of 100 where everyone else is Cain. How many of one kind of faithful must live there before they out-earn the faithless and start to spread? Read across to see the effect of heaven's strength.

And he said, I will not destroy it for ten's sake.Genesis 18:32

A dash means that even 50 of 100 would not be enough. “Assembling together” means the faithful spend an extra 20% of their time with their own kind.

Scattered, under the sun: the reciprocators (Eye for an Eye, Show Me First) need only 5 of 100. Joseph needs 43, because his forgiveness is exploited by a city of Cains. Steadfast, Undulating, Mustard Seed, Prodigal and Lukewarm cannot gain a foothold at all.

Assembling together changes this. Joseph, Eye for an Eye and Show Me First need just one, and the Mustard Seed needs 5. This is Axelrod's cluster result, and it is also Hebrews 10:25. Steadfast faith alone still cannot spread under the sun, but with even a little heaven (s = 0.2) almost every kind needs only one.

Test 8

The storm

Every archetype builds a house: four of its own kind living together. The world starts calm, then for 40 steps chance runs wild and 30% of all moves misfire. Then it is calm again. The line shows the share of moves that keep covenant, and the shaded band is the storm.

And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell not: for it was founded upon a rock.Matthew 7:25

Houses that stood: Steadfast, Joseph, the Prodigal and the Mustard Seed all return to full covenant almost at once. Houses that fell: Eye for an Eye goes from 100% to 49% and never recovers, because once the storm has thrown in a few wrongs, each one is repaid forever. Rocky Ground goes from 100% to nothing: its faith withers in the storm and does not regrow.

Houses that never stood: Thorny Ground's own small betrayals unravel it before any storm comes. In Show Me First's house nobody goes first, so nobody ever sees good faith. The Undulating house sinks by itself under the sun, and under heaven it is held at about half.

How it works

Method and limits

On each move a player's conscience recommends keeping covenant or holding back, according to its rule: always keep it, mirror the partner's last move, wait for proof first, or win-stay lose-shift. It follows a good recommendation with probability equal to its faith (less any greed). When the rule says to retaliate, it shows mercy with probability equal to its faith times its mercy. Under chance, any move can misfire.

Faith then moves. Being treated well raises sight-based faith and being betrayed lowers it, at rates that differ by archetype. Undulating faith also has its own tide. Under heaven, restoration and renewal move faith as described above.

Every result is seeded and averaged over many runs. As Axelrod found, rankings depend on who else is in the field, so the lab lets you change the cast. The heaven results depend on how the promises were modelled, which is why the dial goes from 0 to 1 and each promise can be tested alone. This is a way to think with the parables. It is not a proof about God.
